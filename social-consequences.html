Sydney Housing Watch

Social consequences

The rising housing affordability has significant social consequences on the members of the Sydney community.

Health Impacts

One social impact that the rising housing prices have on members of the Sydney is finacial stress, which can lead to severe physical and psycholigical problems. Concerns for the surge in prices are felt all across Sydney, but low-income families in particular and those who are already experiecing mental health problems tend to have their mental health more heavily impacted by this. Any type of stress has a negative impact on an individual and their body, but financial stress which is prolongded, can have a severe impact on someone's health causing chronic pain, and impacting many other aspects of their life, such as: sleep, apetite, memory, and concentration. The high levels of stress also put individuals at the risk of developing chronic mental illness.
